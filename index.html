01 // EXECUTIVE PROBLEM & MARKET CONTEXT

Transforming Outbound "Push" Friction into Community-Anchored "Pull" Adoption

Anvayaa provides technology-enabled, comprehensive eldercare services to seniors across Indian metros whose children often live abroad or in other states. Prospective families arriving via high-intent queries (e.g., "best eldercare in India") submitted contact forms and were placed into an outbound sales queue.

THE LEGACY MODEL: ASYNCHRONOUS PUSH
Sales Chasing Cold Leads
  • Friction of Silence: Stressed adult children submitted details but waited hours or days for an outbound rep, during which panic peaked and dropped off.
  • High Operational Waste: Sales spent >60% of total hours dialing unanswered calls, chasing invalid numbers, or pitching families who lacked budget.
  • Zero Pre-Call Context: Reps started cold with no knowledge of specific medical history, parent city, or tier preference.
THE TRANSFORMED MODEL: PRODUCT-LED PULL
Self-Serve Intent & Social Proof
  • Immediate Agency: Users instantly preview tiered plans, transparent pricing, and service guardrails immediately upon entering their details.
  • City-Specific Cohorts: Users schedule slots into weekly city onboarding calls hosted live by the Co-Founder, local ground representative, and support rep.
  • Peer Reassurance: Seeing other families in the same metro choose the same plan resolves trust hesitations in a high-stakes domain.
NORTH-STAR METRIC: Qualified Inbound Lead → Onboarded Client Conversion Rate Target: Reduce sales hours per closed family while increasing trust velocity.

01 // THE 0-TO-1 PROBLEM & DISCOVERY FRAMEWORK

Solving the Tab-Toggling Context Switch & Adoption Bottleneck

I joined CoSec Global when the platform was still under active software development. While the core database engine was functional, user engagement stopped prematurely due to critical structural workflow friction.

WORKFLOW FRICTION
The Tab-Toggling Context Switch

Accountants and company secretaries had to leave the portal, open Companies House in a separate browser tab, manually search and download statutory files, and manually re-upload them.

COMMERCIAL BOTTLENECK
The Adoption Bottleneck

Because statutory filing could not occur in-portal, client users saw no incentive to log in daily. We lacked an integrated ICP definition, pricing model, and end-to-end GTM packaging.

Qualitative Prospect Discovery

Conducted ~15 structured interviews across boutique UK accountancy practices and corporate secretarial firms to analyze daily friction, manual verification hours, and data export fatigue.

Regulatory Authority Specifications

Deep-dived into Companies House Authorised Software Provider schemas, mapping strict XML/JSON gateway payload requirements and distinguishing automated tasks from licensed sign-off steps.
